# Validation Brief

> Module 2 · Validation. Frame the riskiest assumption, then build to test it. **This locks your scenario, no switching after M2.**

## Scenario

Scenario 01 · The Retention Engine

## Riskiest assumption

_The one belief that, if false, sinks the idea._

With only 22% of new accounts activating in week one and just 1.4 active seats per account, we assume customers churn because they don't experience meaningful value early enough and adoption doesn't spread beyond the initial buyer.

## Hypothesis

> We believe **A guided week-one activation path that accelerates the core “aha” moment and encourages team adoption.** will cause **More accounts reach first value faster and expand usage across their teams.** for **new B2B SaaS accounts**. We'll know we're right when **Week-one activation increases from 22% to above 40%, time-to-first-value decreases from 6.2 days toward the 1-day target, and 90-day churn decreases from 30%.**.

## Risk type

- [x] Value (do they want it?)
- [ ] Usability (can they use it?)
- [ ] Feasibility (can we build it?)
- [ ] Viability (should we?)

## Kill switch

_The result that would make you stop or pivot._

If the guided week-one activation experience does not improve activation from the 22% baseline or reduce time-to-first-value from 6.2 days, we will assume early activation is not the primary driver of churn and pivot.

## The three ingredients

- **Real data (domain metrics):**
  - 30%, 90-day churn: share of new accounts gone within 3 months.
  - 22%, Activation rate: reach the "aha" action in week one.
  - 1.4, Seats active / account: adoption rarely spreads past the buyer.
  - 6.2 days, Time-to-first-value: median, vs. 1-day target.
  - $1.1M, ARR at risk in the next renewal window.
- **User voice (verbatim quotes):**
  - "I signed up, poked around for ten minutes, and never figured out what it actually did for my team.", Ops lead, churned day 12
  - "Nobody on my team adopted it, so I stopped logging in. It felt like one more tool to babysit.", Eng manager, churned day 47
  - "The value was probably in there somewhere, but I needed it to prove itself in week one, not month three.", Founder, churned day 63
- **Hypothesis (above):** ✓
